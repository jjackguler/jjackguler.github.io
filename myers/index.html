03 · How Sophie fits

Behind your existing lines, never in the way.

Every dealership keeps its number. Your phone system forwards the covered calls to Sophie, and she hands calls back to your staff whenever a person is needed. If Sophie is unavailable, calls fall back to your existing line or voicemail.

CallerDials the store, or a tracking number from your ads. Call tracking is not affected.
Your phone systemForwards after-hours, busy and unanswered calls to Sophie.
Sophie

Answers in English or French, in whichever the caller starts, from information Myers approves.

Transferred to staffBooking request to the storeCallback requestedAnsweredSales lead to the manager
Phase one

Phones

A forwarding rule in your phone system: either all calls, or only busy, unanswered and after-hours calls. When Sophie can't resolve a call, she transfers it to the right extension, and a call summary reaches the staff member by SMS or email.

Systems

Connections, once access is granted

Booking: Xtime, through Cox Automotive's partner program. Customer and tire storage records: your DMS (likely Reynolds), through its certification program; usually the slowest step. Sales leads: into your CRM. Until then: booking requests are emailed to the store, so no call is lost.

Phase two

Websites

One Google Tag Manager tag adds a "Talk to Sophie" button across your sites, alongside the existing chat. Once those contracts end, we recommend one bilingual assistant for every channel.

Data

Privacy by design

Each call opens with a recording notice. Before go-live you receive a data-flow summary: what is stored, by which provider, in which country, and for how long. Where processing takes place outside Canada, it is covered by contractual protections as required under PIPEDA. Retention, access, deletion and incident notification are agreed in writing before go-live.

Investment

How Myers can work out the value

We will not quote a return on investment until we have your numbers. After the pilot, the value per month is:

(appointments completed through Sophie that would otherwise have been missed) × (Myers' average contribution margin per appointment) + (staff time saved) − (cost of the service)

Myers provides the margin figure; we provide the call outcomes. Appointments that would have come through another channel anyway are not counted.

Support. Sophie answers covered calls 24/7; that is an automated service, not 24/7 human support. Our team: business days 8:00–18:00 Eastern, a named contact and a backup, outages acknowledged within 1 hour in those hours.

07 · Why now, and who we are

AI is already arriving at your stores. The question is who it works for.

Your vendors are already putting AI in front of your customers: Gubagoo's AI chat on myers.ca, and Impel's on the Toyota site, both arriving through vendor and manufacturer programs. Each one sees a slice of your customers, in its own system.

The phones are where customers still reach you directly, and where the busiest moments of the year, like tire changeover, are decided. An assistant that answers there, in both official languages, and works for the group as a whole, is a step Myers can own.

We stand beside you

We start with two stores, not twenty. We report every call with an honest outcome. We never let Sophie promise what your systems have not confirmed. And we stay with your teams after go-live, with a named contact, weekly reviews and training when you want it.
